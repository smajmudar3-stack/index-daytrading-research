## Audit: the 2025–2026 "AI-built trading bot" wave versus verifiable track records

Report date: 2026-09-22. Note on method: 30+ web fetches; the web-search budget for this session was exhausted at roughly the 24th query, so the last leg relied on direct fetches of known URLs and local extraction of three research PDFs. Where a primary source was unreachable (X posts, SSRN, a few 403s) I say so and cite the best secondary copy.

---

### 1. The drainers dressed as tutorials, and what regulators have done

**TRM Labs (Sept 2026).** Nine near-identical YouTube tutorials promised a Claude-built ETH arbitrage bot. The videos sent viewers to a fake Remix-style compiler (hosted on S3, Google Cloud Storage and standalone domains); the pasted source was discarded and a drainer bytecode fetched from the operator's server was deployed instead. Between 12 Feb and 11 Aug 2026: **274.60 ETH (~$517,205) from 224 wallets across 234 contracts**, median loss 1 ETH, six collection addresses, 310,474 cumulative views. Any balance over 0.05 ETH auto-swept; a fake error then asked victims to "add 50% of your original liquidity (up to 1 ETH)". Funds went ETH→Dai→bridges→mixer, never touching a centralised exchange. No phishing link, no malicious approval: the victim deploys and funds a contract they own. ([TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/fake-ai-trading-bots-are-getting-victims-to-build-their-own-drainers), [AirdropAlert](https://airdropalert.com/blogs/claude-trading-bot-scam/), [Crypto Briefing, 18 Sep 2026](https://cryptobriefing.com/trm-labs-fake-ai-bot-tutorial-scam/)). AirdropAlert adds the red flags: AI-generated presenters, comment sections seeded by fresh accounts with profit screenshots, and profit claims of "1 ETH every 20 hours" ([Bitbase](https://www.bitbase.com/news/fake-ai-trading-bot-tutorials-steal-274-6-eth-from-224-victims)).

**HP Wolf Security (Sept 2026).** A separate campaign, April–June 2026, advertised an "AI trading agent that trades crypto around the clock" (tradingclaw[.]pro). The installer used a Microsoft-signed binary plus DLL side-loading to run "Needle Stealer", which swapped seven browser wallet extensions (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Phantom among them) for look-alikes that forwarded the typed password ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/17/fake-ai-trading-agent-research/)).

**Regulators.**
- SEC, 22 Dec 2025: three fake crypto platforms and four "AI investment clubs" (AI Wealth Inc., AI Investment Education Foundation Ltd., etc.) recruited via social-media ads into WhatsApp groups pushing "AI-generated investment tips"; **$14M** taken, "no trading ever occurred" ([SEC 2025-144](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025-144-sec-charges-three-purported-crypto-asset-trading-platforms-four-investment-clubs-scheme-targeted)).
- SEC v. Nathan Fuller / Privvy Investments (Texas): an "AI trading bot" promising 40–50% in 30–45 days; **$12.3M** raised, $6.2M spent personally, $5.5M Ponzi payments ([Traders Union](https://tradersunion.com/news/cryptocurrency-news/show/2181324-sec-charges-crypto-ai-fraud-texas/)).
- CFTC customer advisory "AI Won't Turn Trading Bots into Money Machines": red flags are guaranteed returns, 100% win rates, "no experience required", referral bonuses, demo balances shown as real. Anchor case: Mirror Trading International, 23,000 victims, $1.7B in bitcoin, $3.4B judgment ([CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/AITradingBots.html), [CFTC 8854-24](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8854-24)).
- FTC "Operation AI Comply" (Sept 2024): Ascend Ecom ($25M), FBA Machine ($15.9M), Ecommerce Empire Builders: "AI-powered" passive-income schemes rather than trading bots, but the same "AI makes you money while you sleep" pitch ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-announces-crackdown-deceptive-ai-claims-schemes)).

### 2. The engagement-bait pattern, documented

**The "$68 to $750,000" story.** Syndicated copies state the claim verbatim: a 19-year-old Japanese student, bot built in 48 hours with Claude Code, "$6,732 first night", "scans over 50 markets simultaneously", "syncs live BTC data from Binance every second", then "I'm giving away the exact Claude Code setup for free. 24 hours only." Proof offered: none; one placeholder image, no statements, no wallet ([copy of the post](https://storiesoftheendlessarc.blogspot.com/2026/08/he-built-trading-bot-in-48-hoursthen.html)). The original X post was not fetchable. A related viral thread (@beku_AI, 7 Jul 2026, 790K views, 2.2K bookmarks) promises "how to build an automated stock trading system" and funnels to a **"free AI x High-Ticket Affiliate Automation Seminar"**: the "setup" is an affiliate-marketing webinar ([YouMind mirror](https://youmind.com/landing/x-viral-articles/build-stock-trading-bot-claude-code)).

Someone did rebuild what the post describes: three exchanges, ~190 markets, ~1,000 quotes/s, cross-exchange and triangular arbitrage, fee-aware paper trading. Finding: "nothing is net-positive until the fees are switched off" ([OpsToInnovator PR #1](https://github.com/OpsToInnovator/agency-agents/pull/1)).

**The Polymarket "AI quant bot" network.** A dev.to teardown catalogues the template: "retired delivery driver made $8,130 in seven days", "53-year-old from Thailand quietly generated $869,000", dashboards showing "99% win rate over 3,100+ trades", one showing a portfolio of **$1,263,684,453,788.94 and 11,000,000 BTC** (more than exist). Calls to action: "comment Done", "pick a lucky number 29–70" (X Premium engagement pays), and Telegram links (t.me/PolyGunSniperBot, t.me/KreoPolyBot). The payload is the Telegram bot itself: it custodies the wallet and private key. A December 2025 GitHub repo `polymarket-copy-trading-bot` shipped code that read local config files and exfiltrated private keys; the Polycule bot was hacked in Jan 2026 for ~$230K of user funds. ZachXBT's March 2026 exposure documented the playbook: buy aged verified accounts, post synchronised copy, cross-repost, rotate usernames ([dev.to](https://dev.to/__747bb5a1521/the-ai-quant-bot-mirage-inside-the-polymarket-copytrade-scam-network-flooding-crypto-twitter-1856)).

**How the dashboards are made.** The "POLY//TERMINAL" video ($2K to $12K overnight, "70% win rate", "Claude + a device + 1 hour a day") displayed an edge of **8,409,044,361,225%** on an 8-cent gap and was labelled "DEMO COPY" in-frame. Andrew Collins (May 2026) showed the production method: paste a real dashboard screenshot into Claude Artifacts, ask for a copy with your own numbers, export HTML, screen-record. Funnel: tweet → fabricated video → Telegram/GitHub link. StepSecurity found 20+ hijacked bot-repo variants whose npm install step lifted wallet keys and files ([10pm Trader](https://10pmtrader.com/polymarket-bot-scam/)). The scand.ai case file on the "Claude Polymarket arbitrage bot" video notes the bots "frequently mismatched team pairings when comparing bookmaker odds to Polymarket contracts" and that the GitHub links were flagged as credential-theft vectors ([scand.ai](https://scand.ai/scandal/polymarket-claude-ai-arbitrage-bot-controversy)).

So the "setup", when it exists at all, is one of: an affiliate seminar, a Telegram custodial bot, a poisoned repo, or a fake compiler. Zero documented cases where the DM delivered a working, profitable strategy.

### 3. Open-source bots: what their own docs actually claim

| Project | Published live track record? | What the project itself says |
|---|---|---|
| Freqtrade | **No.** | "This software is for educational purposes only… USE THE SOFTWARE AT YOUR OWN RISK." Backtests assume zero slippage, fills at requested price, stoploss-before-ROI within a candle; "past results don't guarantee future success" ([docs](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/), [backtesting](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/backtesting/)) |
| Jesse | **No.** | "We do NOT guarantee profitable trading results in anyways" ([docs](https://docs.jesse.trade/)) |
| Hummingbot | **No per-user P&L.** | Liquidity-mining whitepaper projected "10–50% annually" from *simulation*; the rewards write-ups give no realised APR, no inventory-loss accounting. Pilot: ~70 miners, up to 15% of $ONE volume on Binance; later 1,622 miners, $837M volume, 18 issuers. Volume is published; profit is not ([Hummingbot](https://hummingbot.org/blog/introducing-liquidity-mining-a-marketplace-for-market-makers/), [Demystifying rewards](https://hummingbot.org/blog/demystifying-liquidity-mining-rewards/)) |
| OctoBot | **No.** | Backtest and live share one code path; page unreachable for quotes (403) |
| CloddsBot | **No.** | README lists "118+ strategies", 10 prediction markets, 7 futures exchanges, up to 200x leverage; no P&L, no risk disclaimer ([README](https://github.com/alsk1992/CloddsBot)) |
| Polymarket bots | One honest sample | chudi.dev: 23 real-money trades, 13–16 Feb 2025, 69.6% win rate, **net +$11.51** ($0.50/trade at $5–15 size). Valletta Software's client build reports no numbers and names why edges die: depth, spread/slippage "at sub-cent edges", decay ([chudi.dev](https://chudi.dev/blog/how-i-built-polymarket-trading-bot), [Valletta](https://vallettasoftware.com/blog/post/polymarket-trading-bot-claude-fable-5)) |

Not one of these projects publishes a live equity curve. The gap between "118+ strategies" and "+$11.51" is the whole story.

### 4. Academic base rates

- **Brazil (Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti):** 19,646 first-time mini-Ibovespa day traders, 2013–15. Share with positive net profit falls monotonically with persistence: 29.8% after 1 day, 15.5% at 2–50 days, 8.9%, 6.8%, 5.4%, **3.0% at 300+ days** (n=1,551, so "97% lose"). 1.1% out-earned minimum wage (US$16/day); 0.4% out-earned a bank teller (US$54/day); the single best averaged US$310/day with a standard deviation of US$2,560. "No evidence of learning by day trading." ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101), [Quantpedia summary](https://quantpedia.com/retail-day-trading-is-an-uphill-battle/))
- **Taiwan (Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean), all trades 1992–2006:** ~360,000 people day trade in an average year; ~13–15% profit net of fees in a given year; **fewer than 1% (about 1,000) outperform predictably**. The top 500 ranked on prior-year returns then earn 28.1 bps/day after fees; the bottom cohort loses 34.2 bps/day. Aggregate day-trader returns net of fees were negative in every one of the 15 years. Unprofitable traders with 50+ days' experience still had a 95.3% chance of trading again ([Cross-Section of Speculator Skill](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/Day%20Trading%20Skill%20110523.pdf), [Do Day Traders Rationally Learn](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/Day%20Traders/Day%20Trading%20and%20Learning%20110217.pdf))
- **US retail options (Bryzgalova, Pavlova, Sikorskaya, JF 2023):** retail >60% of options volume; preference for cheap weeklies with an average bid-ask spread of **12.6%**; retail "lose money on average", with aggregate trading costs of ~$6.4B Nov 2019–Jun 2021 ([paper](https://www.sikorskaya.net/files/Bryzgalova_Pavlova_Sikorskaya_2023.pdf), [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jofi.13285))
- **Copy trading:** Pelster & Hofmann (2018) find losses "usually higher for copied trades"; Apesteguia, Oechssler & Weidenholzer (Management Science 2020) find seeing others' success raises risk-taking, more so when copying is one click. eToro's own disclosure: **51% of retail CFD accounts lose money**, and "past performance of an eToro Community Member is not a reliable indicator" ([Wikipedia survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copy_trading), [eToro](https://www.etoro.com/copytrader/risk-warnings/), [Apesteguia et al.](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2019.3508))
- **Polymarket:** loss-rate figures circulating are 70%, 84% and 92.4% of wallets, from different analysts with undisclosed methods; "100,000 accounts booked four-figure losses since 2025" ([Yahoo/Dexter's Lab](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/arbitrage-bots-dominate-polymarket-millions-100000888.html), [10pm Trader](https://10pmtrader.com/polymarket-bot-scam/)). Treat the exact number as soft; the direction is not.

### 5. Any audited retail 10× via automation?

I found **no** case with a third-party audit, broker statements, or tax records. The closest to verifiable are on-chain: Dexter's Lab (Jan 2026) attributes "$313 into $414,000 in a month" to one wallet trading 15-minute BTC/ETH/SOL up/down markets at $4–5K a clip with a 98% win rate, and "$2.2M in two months" to a bot exploiting Polymarket-vs-Binance price lags ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/arbitrage-bots-dominate-polymarket-millions-100000888.html)). What on-chain proves: that an address made that P&L. What it does not prove: who ran it, that $313 was the only capital (funds can be topped up), that it was one wallet out of one rather than one survivor out of hundreds, or that it was a weekend script; a 98% win rate on 15-minute binaries is a latency race against a centralised-exchange feed, which the same article says now resolves in ~2.7 seconds with 73% of profit going to sub-100ms bots. The candid open-source result is +$11.51.

### Verdict

**Five-minute test: real record or graphic?**

1. **Is there a statement or a wallet?** Broker PDF, exchange CSV, or an address you can paste into an explorer. A screenshot or screen-recording is a graphic. Any dashboard can be produced in Artifacts in minutes.
2. **Do the numbers pass arithmetic?** Win rate × trades × average size must reconcile with P&L; edge percentages above a few percent on a prediction market, balances above total coin supply, "8 trillion percent", "$6,732 first night on $68" (a 99× day) fail instantly.
3. **Does it mention costs?** A real record names fees, slippage and drawdown. The honest reproduction of the viral bot found nothing positive with fees on. Fee silence is the tell.
4. **Where does the link go?** Official tool (remix.ethereum.org, the project's own GitHub org) or a "custom compiler", Telegram bot that holds your keys, "free setup for 24 hours", seminar, or exchange referral? The funnel is the product.
5. **Time in market and sample size.** Under 60 sessions or under ~100 trades is noise; this repo's own `flow_tape.study_guard()` refuses studies below 60 sessions for the same reason.
6. **Who else can see it?** Anyone whose results are real can register with a verification service or show a public wallet with dated deposits. Refusal is an answer.

**Honest base rate for "retail bot, 5 months, 10×":** 10× over ~105 trading days is 2.2% per day compounded. The best 500 of 360,000 Taiwanese day traders, selected *after* a winning year, earned 28.1 bps/day after fees, which compounds to roughly +34% in five months, not +900%. Fewer than 1% of that population outperformed at all; 97% of persistent Brazilian day traders lost money; the one open-source bot that published real trades made $11.51. Put together: the probability that a randomly encountered "AI bot did 10× in 5 months" post is a real, reproducible track record is well under 1%, and the probability that its link takes your money is, on the 2026 evidence, materially higher than the probability that it makes you any.
