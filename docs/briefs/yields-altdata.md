## Verdict first

Nothing in Part A or Part B gets near 58% a month. The best documented after-cost figure in either part is roughly 6-12% a year, and the routes advertising more than 15% a year have a recorded history of losing principal. 20% a year is not on offer from any yield route; it is only plausible from a signal with real edge, and the best-documented ones below sit at 0.5-1% a month before decay.

**Every documented retail "yield" above 15% and what it cost.** The 2022 CeFi lenders (Celsius, Voyager, BlockFi, Genesis, FTX) advertised 7.4-9%, up to 17%; $46.5B and 4.3M users went into bankruptcy, and Celsius owed $4.7B to 1.7M users against $4.3B of assets ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/231022/ftx-celsius-voyager-blockfi-genesis-collapse-fed-report-charts), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/07/17/how-the-fall-of-celsius-dragged-down-crypto-investors.html)). Anchor paid ~20% on UST until UST was worth nothing. Yieldstreet's marine and real-estate notes have $208M of cumulative losses, a $1.9M SEC settlement and a $6.2M class action ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yieldstreet)). Short-volatility "yield" is the last one: the classic Coval-Shumway figure is 26%/yr with a 1.16 Sharpe, but that sample is 1986-1995 and put sellers have recorded -800% months ([Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/strategies/volatility-risk-premium-effect)); the Chicago Fed's 2025 paper finds index option alphas indistinguishable from zero since about 2010 ([Dew-Becker & Giglio](https://www.chicagofed.org/-/media/publications/working-papers/2025/wp2025-17.pdf?sc_lang=en)). That last one matters for this repo: the plain variance premium the 0DTE condor was meant to harvest has been measured as gone for fifteen years.

## Part B: alternative data and ML a retail quant can actually pull

| Signal and data | Access | Published result | Measured decay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google search volume (Da/Engelberg/Gao 2011) | Google Trends, free, weekly, 5 terms per query | Russell 3000, 2004-08: high abnormal SVI earns +11 bps/week for two weeks (~5.7%/yr), then reverses within a year; momentum 2.45% stronger in high-SVI stocks | Bijl et al. 2016, 2008-13: sign reversed, high search predicts lower returns; profitable before costs, not after ([IRFA](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2016.03.015)) |
| Reddit WSB sentiment | Reddit API (rate-limited; Pushshift is gone), free | Bradley/Hanousek/Jame/Xiao: WSB due-diligence posts had value pre-2021 and lost it after the GameStop event ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3806921)); 2025 backtests claim 70-84% outperformance in 2021/2023 with no transaction costs and no out-of-sample split ([arXiv 2508.02089](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.02089)); GME/AMC study found comment volume and Google Trends beat sentiment ([arXiv 2507.22922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22922)) | Post-2021 collapse documented; attention studies show reversal ([ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1057521924006537)) |
| StockTwits bull/bear (Cookson/Niessner) | StockTwits API, free | Disagreement robustly forecasts abnormal volume, not returns ([NBER draft](https://conference.nber.org/confer/2016/BFs16/Cookson_Niessner.pdf), [SSRN 2023 daily measure](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4529594)) | Never a return signal, so nothing to decay |
| 10-K tone (Loughran/McDonald) | EDGAR full-text search and XBRL frames API, free; LM dictionary free | Negative-word share relates to filing-period returns and volatility ([JF 2011](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01625.x)); effect is a same-window reaction, not a tradable lead | Now a standard input at every quant shop |
| LLM-scored headlines (Lopez-Lira/Tang) | Free headlines plus any LLM | 90% hit rate on the non-tradable first reaction; predicts subsequent drift mainly in small stocks and negative news; "strategy returns decline as LLM adoption rises" ([arXiv 2304.07619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07619)) | Authors measure the decay themselves |
| Job postings (Lev/Wu 2022) | Company career pages, free to scrape | High-posting firms stay in the top valuation group, low-posting firms drop ([SSRN 4112800](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4112800)) | No alpha number published that I could verify |
| App downloads (Lin/Liu/Lu) | Free store charts only; full data is paid | One SD rise in amusement-app downloads over four weeks predicts +49 bps market return over the next four weeks, across countries ([SSRN 4744745](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4744745)) | New; unmeasured |
| Options-implied (earnings variance premium) | Chain snapshots, this repo already has them | Repo's own result: rich-percentile pre-earnings straddles gave a +3.63pt seller edge vs -4.30pt for cheap, n=266, t=9.07 | Index-level VRP measured as zero since 2010 (above); the earnings-window version is the one with life |
| Cross-sectional ML (Gu/Kelly/Xiu) | Free CRSP substitutes are poor; needs paid data | 1987-2016 out-of-sample long-short Sharpe 1.35 value-weighted, 2.45 equal-weighted; best predictors are momentum, liquidity, volatility ([RFS](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/5/2223/5758276)) | Avramov/Cheng/Metzker: gains concentrate in microcaps and distressed names and mostly vanish after costs; a faithful replication needs 32 cores and 192 GB ([Tidy Finance](https://blog.tidy-finance.org/posts/gu-kelly-xiu-replication/)) |

The base rate for all of these: McLean and Pontiff's 82 published anomalies averaged 42.8 bps/month in sample, 78% of that out of sample, 51% after publication ([paper](https://www.fmg.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2020-08/Jeffrey-Pontiff.pdf)). Quantpedia's 355 backtested strategies lost 33% of Sharpe on average and 44% at the median out of sample ([Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/in-sample-vs-out-of-sample-analysis-of-trading-strategies/)). Halve any number in the table before believing it.

## The two or three worth testing, and the exact pull

1. **Earnings-window variance premium, structure only.** Best-documented after-cost edge, and it is this repo's own measurement, not a paper. Expected: roughly +3-4 points per event on rich-percentile names, at maybe 20-40 events a month if the universe is the S&P 1500 with liquid weeklies. Pull: for every earnings date in the next 60 sessions, snapshot the front straddle at the close before the print (bid and ask, not mid), the UW percentile of implied move, and the realised open-to-open move. `05_studies/earnings_vrp_test.py` already does the scoring; what is missing is a forward paper ledger through `weekly_book` so the +3.63 is measured live rather than in history.

2. **LLM-scored 8-K and headline drift in small caps.** Lopez-Lira and Tang's drift is concentrated where retail can still trade and institutions cannot bother: Russell 2000 minus microcaps, negative news. No after-cost number is published, so budget a 40-60 bps round trip and treat anything under 1%/month gross as noise. Pull: EDGAR full-text search for 8-K items 2.02, 4.02, 5.02 and 8.01 filed after the close, score with a fixed prompt, hold day 1 to day 5, market cap between $300M and $3B. Two hundred filings gets a first read.

3. **Amusement-app downloads as a monthly market-timing overlay.** 49 bps per four weeks is about 6%/yr, one trade a month, index ETF, near-zero cost, and it has not been published long enough to have been arbitraged. The catch is data: Apptopia and Sensor Tower free tiers do not give download counts. The test is whether free Google Play top-grossing rank churn for games proxies the paper's series; if the correlation is under 0.5, drop it.

Google Trends and Reddit are not on the list on purpose: the one after-cost test of search volume came back negative, and the WSB literature's own authors date the death of the signal to January 2021.
