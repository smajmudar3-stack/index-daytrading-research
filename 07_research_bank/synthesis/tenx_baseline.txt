==========================================================================================
HOW NEGATIVE EXPECTANCY EATS THE 10% CEILING
==========================================================================================
  Bold play: each bet risks everything staked for a multiple. With
  per-bet expectancy e, n sequential bets each needing 10^(1/n):

   bets   each must return      e=+0%     e=-5%    e=-15%    e=-30%
      1             10.00x     10.00%     9.50%     8.50%     7.00%
      2              3.16x     10.00%     9.02%     7.22%     4.90%
      3              2.15x     10.00%     8.57%     6.14%     3.43%
      5              1.58x     10.00%     7.74%     4.44%     1.68%
     10              1.26x     10.00%     5.99%     1.97%     0.28%
     20              1.12x     10.00%     3.58%     0.39%     0.01%

  Read down a column: MORE BETS IS STRICTLY WORSE once expectancy
  is negative. Each additional trade pays the house again. At e=-15%
  twenty bets is a rounding error away from zero.

  Read across a row: this is the cost of the spread. One bet at
  -30% still leaves 7%; twenty bets at -30% leaves almost nothing.
